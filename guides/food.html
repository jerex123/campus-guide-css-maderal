Eating Well on a Student Budget

Your first years is busy; New classes, New friends, and long days on Campus. It is easy to skip meals or spend your whole allowance on milk tea. Eating regularly Simple meals help you focus in class, and you do not need a big budget to do it.

Where to Eat Near Campus

You have more options than you think.here are usual go-to spots:

  • The school canteen for quick rice meals between classes
  • Carinderias outside the gate for cheap,filling ulam and rice
  • Your own baon from home,which is the cheapest choice of all
  • Convenience stores for emergency snacks,not for every meal

How to Plan Your Weekly Food Budget

A Little planning on your sunday saves you from being broke by thursday:

  • Check how much allowance you have for the whole week.
  • Set aside money for fare and school needs first..
  • Pack baon on at least two days to stretch your budget.L
  • Keep a small amount for emergencies, like a surprise projec

Quick Tips

Always bring a refilable water bottle.water station on campus are free,and you will save a lot compared to buying bottle every day.
